第一次考试 / First Game

新手指导 / Quick Guide

你正在参加一场期末考试。目标不是拿高分，而是让自己的风险最低。

You are taking a final exam. Your goal is not a high score. Your goal is the lowest risk.

1. 如何获胜 / How To Win

游戏结束时，4 张手牌数字总和，加上监考奖励或惩罚，再加上作弊检查惩罚，得到最终 Risk Score。最低分获胜。

At the end, add your 4 card values, Hall Monitor modifiers, and the Cheating Check penalty. The lowest Risk Score wins.

2. 开局记忆 / Opening Memory

你有 4 张盖着的牌。开局你已经看过前 2 张，标着“已记住 / Known”的牌就是你知道的牌。

You have 4 facedown cards. At the start, you know your first 2 cards. Cards marked “Known” are remembered.

3. 你的回合 / Your Turn

先点抽牌堆或弃牌堆。拿到 1-9 可以换进自己的手牌，或直接弃掉。

First choose the draw pile or discard pile. If you take a 1-9 card, swap it into your hand or discard it.

4. 技能牌 / Skill Cards

10-13 是技能牌：10 偷看自己，11 偷看别人，12 交换牌，13 翻硬币做监考检查。红面被抓 +2，绿面安全 -3。

Cards 10-13 are skill cards: 10 peeks at your card, 11 peeks at another player, 12 swaps cards, and 13 flips a Hall Monitor coin. Red means caught +2, green means safe -3.

5. 交卷 / Declare Done

如果你觉得自己的风险已经够低，回合结束后可以宣布 Done。其他玩家各走最后 1 回合，然后结算。

When your risk feels low enough, declare Done after your turn. Every other player gets one final turn, then scoring begins.

6. 小建议 / Tip

尽量用低数字换掉高数字，同时记住自己的暗牌。低分很香，但太早交卷可能给 AI 留机会。

Replace high numbers with low numbers and remember your hidden cards. Low risk is great, but declaring too early gives AI a chance.
